Two independent failure mechanisms

Shared parser: for day-first tenant locales, the shared parser reads month first. For example, entering 07/04/2026 intending 7 April is interpreted as 4 July. The supplied classifier places app/email reports and booking-v2 form reports here. Track the repair in engineering issue #1.

booking-v3 hint: this form prints MM/DD/YYYY even for day-first tenants, while interpreting the input the tenant’s way. Following the hint with 08/06/2026 intending 6 August instead produces 8 June. The relevant tenants are alder.physio (de-DE), copper.studio (es-ES), and pale.harbour (pt-PT). Track this separate repair in engineering issue #2.

These examples follow the supplied mechanisms; they are not results of running the production booking service. The exported code is evidence for this classification, not a production patch or an independent reproduction environment.

Proposed triage rule: compare causes, not subjects

Resolve the tenant, read its locale and booking-form version, and check the channel. Classify with causeOf; merge only when sameCause confirms the same known cause. Do not auto-merge an unknown tenant or an unplaced report. A subject is a search aid, not the duplicate key.

  • Different subjects, same cause: kettle.books app versus email belong together.
  • Same subject and tenant, different causes: alder.physio form (booking-v3 hint) versus app (shared parser) must stay separate.
  • Same subject and channel, different tenants: copper.studio form versus morrow.tax form must stay separate because their form versions differ.
  • Different subjects, same cause: pale.harbour form and alder.physio form can merge.
  • Unknown tenant: identical text alone does not establish a known shared cause.
